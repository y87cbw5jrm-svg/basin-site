# Basin

A market dynamics observatory for the S&P 1500 and the S&P International 700 (the
S&P Global 1200 outside the US): attractors and flow, renormalization, information
and transfer entropy, regimes, early warnings, CEO backgrounds, and shovel-seller
and trend filters with point-in-time backtests.

Open the site: https://y87cbw5jrm-svg.github.io/basin-site/

This repository holds only the built website and its precomputed data (prices from
Yahoo Finance, fundamentals and proxy statements from SEC EDGAR, constituents from
Wikipedia, SPDR fund holdings and iShares fund filings (SEC Form N-PORT) mapped to
listings with OpenFIGI, biographies from Wikidata), as of the snapshot dates shown
in the site's sidebar. The S&P International 700 is rebuilt from those public
sources (S&P's own lists are not public); its TOPIX 150 part is approximated by the
150 largest holdings of the iShares MSCI Japan ETF. It is not investment advice.
